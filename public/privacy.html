Privacy note

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Your inquiry is used to reply to you and plan your event. You won’t be added to a mailing list, and your details are never sold.

Who this is

This page belongs to Tyler Pigott, a speaker and workshop leader in Gig Harbor, Washington.

What the form collects

What you type: your name, email, organization and role, event date and city, format, audience size, budget, talk of interest, your message, and whether you’re working with a StoryBrand representative. If you add an event coordinator or EA email, that person gets a copy of your inquiry. The form also notes which version of this page you saw and the link that brought you here, if it was tagged.

How it travels

The form is sent by email to Tyler through FormSubmit, a form-processing service.

Who sees it

Tyler. He shares details only as needed to plan your event, for example with the coordinator you copy or the StoryBrand representative you mention.

Analytics and other services

Google Analytics cookies measure how this page is used, such as visits, clicks and how far people scroll. No names, emails or messages are sent to it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the video loads from Vimeo only when you press play.

How long it’s kept

Your inquiry stays in Tyler’s email and contact records for as long as it’s needed for your inquiry and any booking.

Seeing or deleting your details

Email Tyler@pigottfam.com and he’ll show you what he has or delete it.
