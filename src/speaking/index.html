Your team knows what you do. Your customers still don’t.

Customers rarely complain about a confusing message. They just pick the competitor who’s easier to understand.

  • Ask ten people what you do and you’ll hear ten answers.

    Leaders, managers and frontline teams each explain the company their own way, so customers hear noise.

  • Your teams lead with features and customers tune out.

    Presentations, pitches and sales calls open with the product when they should open with the customer’s problem, and decisions stall.

  • The marketing gets louder and the results stay flat.

    Websites, decks and campaigns fill up with insider language that customers have to stop and translate.

A great company shouldn’t lose customers because nobody can explain it simply.

Today he’s VP at StoryBrand, where he leads growth, strategy and the global network of StoryBrand Certified marketing consultants. He brings 25+ years in marketing and sales, from launching a national beverage product to building teams at a biotech startup.

Questions planners ask.

What does it cost?

In-person keynotes start at $25,000 plus travel. Virtual keynotes and half-day and full-day workshops are quoted to fit your event. Planning ahead? You can hold your date with a deposit, or tell Tyler your timing and he’ll let you know how far ahead he books.

Will Tyler customize the talk for our audience?

Yes. Before your event he learns your industry, your audience and your goals, then rebuilds the examples around your world so the framework lands.

Will this work for a complex or technical business?

Yes. Tyler’s clients include tech distributors, industrial manufacturers, banks and network companies. The more jargon your business runs on, the more your people get out of a session about plain words.

Does this work at a conference where attendees come from different companies?

Yes. Each person works on their own business’s one sentence, so it fits a ballroom of different companies as well as a single team.

Will Tyler pitch StoryBrand from the stage?

No. Your session is about your business. If your team wants more help afterward, that’s a conversation for later, on your terms.

Why book Tyler instead of Donald Miller?

Don created the StoryBrand Framework, and if you want the author, he’s a great choice. Tyler leads growth at StoryBrand and built a company on the framework before he joined. You get the same framework, taught by someone who used it to make payroll, and you work with Tyler directly from the first call to the follow-up.

What audience sizes does he work with?

Everything from a leadership team around one table to a full conference ballroom. Workshops work best when teams can sit together at tables and write.

Can he present virtually?

Yes. Virtual keynotes are built to keep remote audiences taking part, with live writing and Q&A. Tell Tyler about your event in the form and he’ll send the fee with your proposal.

Can we record the session for internal use?

Ask Tyler on the fit call. Tell him how you’ll use it (new-hire onboarding, for example), and he’ll confirm what’s possible.

Where does he travel from?

Tyler lives in Gig Harbor, Washington, near Seattle, and travels anywhere.

What do you need from us on the day?

A microphone and a screen with an HDMI connection. A confidence monitor and Wi-Fi help. Tyler brings his own laptop and a PDF backup. Workshops need tables and room for teams to write.

We’re a StoryBrand customer. How do we book?

Talk to your StoryBrand representative, or use the form below and check the StoryBrand representative box. Either way, you’re booking the same Tyler.
